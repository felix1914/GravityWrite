\n

The 2-Minute Rule is an effective strategy for tackling easier questions first. By quickly identifying and answering questions that take two minutes or less, you can build confidence and save time for more challenging problems. This approach aligns with the advice to \"tackle the easiest questions first to build confidence and save time for more challenging ones.\"
